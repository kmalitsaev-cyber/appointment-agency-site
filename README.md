# Appointment Setting Training Website

A single-page site: hero, services, free content (YouTube videos + Google Drive guides), Google Form email signup, and a Book a call button linked to Calendly.

Everything lives in `index.html`. Search for `EDIT` comments to find the parts to customize:

- **Your name / brand**: the logo in the nav and the footer.
- **YouTube videos**: replace `VIDEO_ID_1`, `VIDEO_ID_2` with your video IDs.
- **Google Drive guides**: replace `YOUR_GUIDE_LINK_*` with share links (set to "Anyone with the link").
- **Google Form**: replace `YOUR_FORM_ID` with your form's embed URL (Send > `< >` tab).
