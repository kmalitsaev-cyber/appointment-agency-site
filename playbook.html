The DM Setting Playbook

How to take a fitness lead from a cold DM to a booked sales call, in 7 stages. Follow them in order and don't skip ahead.

Stage 1

Outreach: build rapport

Open on something from their profile, not fitness.

Opener"Yo where's this man? Looks sick" or "Fire view bro, where's this?"
After they reply"Nice man, you based there?" / "Fire bro, what brought you out there?" / "Damnn been planning on heading out there for a minute actually, you based there?" / "Oh sick, my guys @XXX and @XXX live there, been planning on going to meet them for a minute now"
Rule: keep the rapport going until you can move into fitness talk naturally, then follow the stages below.
Stage 2

Pre-qualify

Pick these up casually while you chat:

  • Where they're from
  • How old they are
  • What they do for work
Stage 3

Goals and why they matter

  • What are you actually looking to achieve with your fitness, man?
  • Why do you want to hit [goal]?
  • What would hitting [goal] do for you?
  • How long have you wanted to hit [goal]?
  • Why now? What's got you motivated?
Tip: put a number on their goal. If you didn't get their job earlier, relate it back to fitness here. Only ask for a timeframe if they're an outbound lead; inbound leads are usually already high intent.
Stage 4

The struggle

  • What sort of hurdles are you facing with achieving [goal]?
  • Why do you feel like you haven't lost that weight yet?
  • What's stopping you?
Never accept time as an excuse. Everyone has time. Ask: "Is it genuinely not having 30 minutes a day, or is there something else there?"
If they say nothing's wrong (outbound)"I get you bro, but I know people with that busy a schedule who see results in [their timeframe]. So what's really stopping you?"
Stage 5

Timeframe and emotional impact

  • What does consistency look like for you? What's stopping you right now?
  • How long have you been struggling with this?
  • [X] years is quite a long time, man. How's that been sitting with you?
Time objection"If all you needed was an extra 30 minutes a day on your health, diet and routine, and you'd genuinely see that [goal] version of yourself, would you do it?"
Stage 6

Future pace

Use Heaven if they've shared a lot of pain. Use Hell if they haven't.

Heaven"If you do fix [struggle] and get to [goal], what sort of impact do you see that having on your day-to-day life, man?"
Hell"If you continue to be [struggle] for the next [how long they've struggled], what sort of impact do you see it having?"
Skip this stage if they're already a good prospect.
Stage 7

Pitch the call

Offer"Well listen, if you'd be open to it, I know I can help with [their pain + goal]."
Investment check"Obviously a personalised plan does require an investment to get started, so just to get a rough idea, are you in a place where that would feel comfortable for you?"
Then ask for the call. If money comes up and their job is good: "What do you think is genuinely more expensive, investing in yourself or living another [X years] feeling [struggle]?"

Quick checklist

  1. Goal
  2. What would hitting it do for you?
  3. How long have you wanted it?
  4. Why is it important now?
  5. Hurdles or struggles
  6. How long?
  7. How's that been sitting with you?
  8. Heaven or Hell (skip for good prospects)
  9. "I know I can help with…"
  10. Investment check
  11. Call request
